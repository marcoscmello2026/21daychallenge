A real-life example

Day 9 — The 10-Minute Conversation

"Many couples don't stop communicating. They stop talking about anything other than responsibilities."

Each day begins with a brief idea like this one, followed by a specific action and questions to guide the conversation never vague tips like "talk more."

  • What has been taking up most of your emotional energy lately?
  • Is there anything I could understand better about you right now?
  • What did we stop doing that you miss?
